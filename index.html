Our Manifesto

Software has no
borders.

"If an exceptional app is locked in a walled garden, we reverse-engineer a native, high-performance twin for everyone else."
  1. 01

    Identify the locked gem

    We seek out the best apps that are trapped inside a single ecosystem — tools that users on other platforms deserve but can't access.

  2. 02

    Reverse-engineer the experience

    We study each feature in depth and rebuild it natively — not a wrapper, not a port. A true, platform-first twin built from scratch.

  3. 03

    Ship it for everyone

    We release high-performance software to Windows, Linux, and beyond — making no distinction between ecosystems. Great software belongs everywhere.

  4. 04

    Stay independent, always

    RavenLabs will never be acquired by a platform it is fighting against. We are a collective first, a company second.
